## 3. Interactive Mouse Parallax & Proximity Zoom (Task 3)

**Test Scenario**:
1. **Resting Pose**: Pointer at $(x: 200, y: 300)$ on the left side. Pouch remains at default resting scale ($0.98$) with subtle procedural micro-bobbing.
2. **Pointer Approach**: Cursor moves across the screen towards the 3D pouch $(x: 1040, y: 450)$.
3. **Proximity Zoom**: Proximity factor triggers smooth scale enlargement from $0.98 \to 1.045$, revealing fine quilted leatherette detail.
4. **Rotational Parallax**: Pouch exhibits gentle $3.4^\circ$ ($0.06\,\mathrm{rad}$) yaw and $-2.3^\circ$ pitch tracking pointer coordinates.
5. **Pointer Departure**: Pointer moves back to the left side.
6. **Smooth Return**: Damped exponential interpolation (`delta * 3.0`) glides the product back to its resting orientation and scale without snapping or jitter.

## 4. Four Synchronized Color Environments (Task 4)

**Transition Fidelity**: Zero white flash; smooth 1000ms CSS and Three.js color lerp across background gradient, PBR material, key light, fill light, and ambient glow.

## 5. Scroll Depth Transition (Task 5)

**Test Scenario**:
1. Page scrolled from `top: 0` to `top: 380px`.
2. GSAP `ScrollTrigger` smoothly scales the right-side stage from $1.00 \to 0.92$, increases $y$ offset by $+28\mathrm{px}$, and dims opacity to $0.88$.
3. Product visually appears to recede deeper into the studio floor as the user enters the Manufacturing Divisions section.
4. Native page scrolling is 100% unhijacked and responsive.
5. Scrolling back to top smoothly restores full scale ($1.00$), resting elevation, and full opacity.
