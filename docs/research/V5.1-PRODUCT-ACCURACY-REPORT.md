## 1. Task 1: AR-002 Product Identity & Image Disassociation Audit

### 1.1 The Failure Mode
In earlier revisions, the catalogue entry for **AR-002: Two-Tone Contrast Base Vanity Pouch** displayed `image copy 23.png`. 
- **Physical Product Discrepancy:** `image copy 23.png` depicts a black velvet evening clutch with a longitudinal gold zipper stripe and rectangular puller.
- **Product Title Mismatch:** The catalogue specification defines AR-002 as a structured two-tone contrast-base vanity case with a top carry handle and wrap-around zipper.
- **Third-Party Trademark Risk:** The original source photograph for AR-002 (`image copy 27.png`) features an unverified third-party mark ("RAYS"). Under Section 29 of the Indian Trade Marks Act, 1999, public commercial display of third-party registered trademarks without written license creates actionable legal liability.

3. **High-Resolution Editorial Placeholder:** Generated and deployed `public/images/products/AR-002-pending.png` and `public/images/products/AR-002.png` (77,218 bytes):
   - Background: Warm Alabaster (`#FAF8F5`).
   - Graphic: Minimalist architectural line blueprint illustrating the two-tone split seam, top handle, and wrap-around zipper.
   - Typography: *"ARMANO STUDIO SPECIFICATION — Product photograph pending client approval"*.
4. **Catalogue & Discovery Badges:** Added `"Photo Pending Approval"` badges on [`CatalogueView.jsx`](file:///c:/Users/AFFAN%20KHAN/OneDrive/Desktop/ARMANO/src/components/catalogue/CatalogueView.jsx) and [`ProductDiscoverySection.jsx`](file:///c:/Users/AFFAN%20KHAN/OneDrive/Desktop/ARMANO/src/sections/home/ProductDiscoverySection.jsx).
