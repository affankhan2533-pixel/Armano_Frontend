## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Investigation

The client and user identified that the previous procedural 3D model looked "too smooth, blocky and artificial" and resembled an inflated balloon toy rather than a tailored luxury cosmetic pouch. Additionally, earlier captures on localhost showed zoomed-in cropping or blank regions.

Under **V7.2**, the 3D asset has been mathematically re-engineered from the authentic AR-009 physical reference photograph into a soft-goods tailored visualisation:
1. **Natural Body Proportions**: Replaced flat rectangular geometry with a tailored standing wedge ($2.44 \times 1.58$, base depth $0.82$, top depth $0.20$), featuring a gentle central crest sag ($0.026$) and rounded shoulder falloff ($0.14$).
2. **Refined Down-Filled Quilting**: Eliminated balloon-like math domes; replaced with an authentic 5-column $\times$ 4-row pillowed cushion matrix featuring distinct stitch grooves ($0.022$ trench depth) and delicate radiating corner tension creases ($0.003$ amplitude).
3. **Continuous Tailored Piping Welt**: Generated two continuous 3D tube welts ($0.012$ radius) framing the perimeter of the front and back quilted panels, providing authentic atelier craftsmanship contours.
4. **Wrap-Around Volumetric Gusset**: Enhanced the side and bottom wrap with an 8-ring lofted profile and natural internal foam fullness ($0.024$ lateral bow).
5. **Authentic Left-Edge Zipper Slider & Puller**: Relocated the champagne gold zipper slider and rectangular metal puller tab with central cutout to the far left edge, exactly matching the AR-009 photograph.
6. **Tactile Fabric Bump Synthesis**: Upgraded procedural bump map to $512 \times 512$ with diagonal twill weave grain, organic micro-creases, and tactile fiber stippling.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis of Previous Rendering Issues

| Issue Observed | Technical Root Cause | V7.2 Remediation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zoomed-In Cropping (User Image 1)** | Camera FOV/position in an earlier full-bleed background experiment was pushed too close, and OrbitControls zoom was enabled. Pointer interaction distorted the camera distance. | Locked desktop camera distance to `targetZ = 4.6` and mobile to `5.15` via `AdaptiveCameraRig`. Explicitly set `enableZoom={false}` and `enablePan={false}` on `OrbitControls`. Pointer proximity zoom clamped between `0.98` and `1.045`. |
| **Blank Hero Canvas (User Image 2)** | In Brave Browser, Brave Shields "Fingerprint Protection" blocks WebGL context creation. React Three Fiber threw an unhandled context exception, unmounting `<Hero3DCanvas>` completely. | Implemented proactive WebGL capability detection via `useSyncExternalStore(noopSubscribe, getWebGLSupport)`. Added `CanvasErrorBoundary` and an ultra-high-resolution studio packshot fallback (`AR-009.png`). Positioned swatches outside the canvas boundary so they are 100% accessible under all browser configurations. |
| **Mobile Unmounted State During Metric Resizing** | Changing CDP device metrics dynamically in Chrome without page reload caused the Next.js dynamic client component to miscalculate container boundaries. | Enforced clean responsive breakpoints using both viewport width and canvas dimensions. In automated testing, each viewport navigates cleanly, confirming 100% WebGL mount at 360px, 390px, and 430px. |

## 3. Files Changed & Architectural Enhancements

### 1. `src/components/3d/QuiltedPouchMesh.jsx`
- Upgraded resolution from $48 \times 38$ to **$80 \times 64$ segments** ($5,184$ vertices per quilted face).
- Re-computed front and back panels with the new organic quilting formula (soft plateau dome, recessed stitch trench, and corner tension micro-creases).
- Added `frontPipingGeo` and `backPipingGeo` via `THREE.CatmullRomCurve3` and `TubeGeometry` for perimeter piping.
- Added 8-ring lofted wrap gusset with organic fullness.
- Added articulated zipper slider and rectangular puller tab with central slot anchored to the left crest.
- Synthesized high-definition $512 \times 512$ twill weave bump texture.
- Applied `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with soft-touch sheen (`sheen: 0.55`, `sheenColor: '#FFFFFF'`, `clearcoat: 0.08`, `roughness: 0.50`).

### 2. `src/components/3d/Hero3DCanvas.jsx`
- Added upward floor bounce light (`position: [0, -2.5, 1.8]`) in `StudioLighting` to soften underside shadows on the tailored welt and base.
- Refined `AdaptiveCameraRig` to smoothly adapt camera distance across mobile ($Z=5.15$) and desktop ($Z=4.6$).
- Added `IntersectionObserver` to pause the WebGL render loop (`frameloop={isInView ? 'always' : 'never'}`) whenever the hero is scrolled out of the viewport, reducing idle GPU consumption to zero.
- Capped device pixel ratio to `dpr={[1, 1.75]}` for retina sharpness without mobile thermal throttling.
- Updated footnote to differentiate between interactive 3D concept mode and authentic reference sample fallback.

## 6. Remaining Limitations & Notes

- **Procedural Model Nature**: The 3D model is a high-fidelity procedural representation developed to demonstrate colorway concepts and tactile finishes. Physical production counter-samples are always verified during counter-sampling at the Mumbai facility.
- **Brave Browser Shields**: On browsers configured with aggressive fingerprint blocking, the proactive WebGL detector automatically falls back to the high-resolution photographic packshot (`AR-009.png`) with ambient drop shadows, ensuring the product is never invisible.
