## 1. Executive Summary

In accordance with strict brand accuracy protocols and client image governance directives, a comprehensive audit and remediation was conducted across the ARMANO homepage storefront. All misleading category imagery, unsupported manufacturing claims, and unconfirmed technical specifications have been eliminated.

### Core Remediation Achievements:
1. **Collections Image Governance (`CategoryShowcaseSection.jsx`)**:
   - **01 Cosmetic & Skincare Pouches**: Retained verified, authentic client prototype packshot (`AR-009.png`).
   - **02 Tote Bags & Carry-Alls**: Retained authentic workshop prototype (`sample-tote.png` from PDF Page 7). Avoided misclassifying AR-011.
   - **03 Corporate & Luxury Gifting**: Completely removed the unverified black evening clutch photograph (`sample-velvet-clutch.png`). Implemented an elegant, minimal typography visual panel detailing client tech-pack programmes and provisional tiers.
   - **04 Custom OEM / ODM Manufacturing**: Completely removed the heart-shaped pouch on cutting bench (`AR-013-workbench.png`). Implemented an elegant, architectural atelier typography visual panel detailing pattern engineering from CAD and Mumbai workshop execution.
2. **Elimination of Fabricated Technical Blueprint Numbers**:
   - In `ManufacturingStorySection.jsx` and `ManufacturingIntroSection.jsx`, removed all unsupported figures:
     * `Width: 240mm`
     * `Height: 155mm`
     * `Gusset: 85mm`
     * `Tolerance: ±2.0mm`
     * `Seam allowance: 8mm`
   - Replaced with the required clean editorial visual block:
     ```
     CLIENT TECH-PACK
     PRODUCT DIMENSIONS
     MATERIAL & CONSTRUCTION
     PENDING CONFIRMATION
     ```
3. **Truthful Manufacturing Claims**:
   - Removed all unsupported claims across the homepage:
     * *"Electroplated hardware"*
     * *"Guaranteed zipper glide"*
     * *"Dual-stage inline QC"*
     * *"Guaranteed material durability"*
     * *"Reinforced structural engineering"*
     * *"Specific lining denier"*
   - Replaced with truthful, observable descriptions labeled `VISUAL OBSERVATION // PROTOTYPE DETAIL`.
4. **Material Story Cleaned (`ProductDetailMacroGallery.jsx`)**:
   - Card 01: `Quilted Surface Appearance`
   - Card 02: `Woven Textile Appearance`
   - Card 03: `Visible Hardware Details`
   - Card 04: `Observed Pouch Structure`
5. **Campaign Copy Cleaned (`EditorialCampaignBanner.jsx`)**:
   - Retained close-up campaign photography (`AR-009-puffer.png`).
   - Replaced fabricated claims with concise, truthful B2B wording.
6. **Strict Preservation of Approved Baseline**:
   - Approved V7.2.1 3D hero remains 100% frozen.
   - Master Product Gallery (13 master SKUs) with AR-002 "Photo Pending Approval" badge strictly preserved.
   - Zero modifications to RFQ backend, Supabase, security, or routes.
