## 1. Executive Brief & Art Direction Guidelines

To align future product packshots with the quiet luxury aesthetic benchmarks demonstrated in the approved ARMANO design system (and reference benchmarks such as the *Maison* vanity case and *Luxury Skin Care* editorial framework), all future studio photography must adhere to the standardized specifications below.

### 1.1 Aesthetic Art Direction Standard
- **Backdrop & Staging Sweep**: Seamless, untextured warm ivory sweep (`#F7F4EF` to `#F4EFEA`) or warm light alabaster. Avoid glossy reflective acrylic or harsh artificial colored sweeps (e.g., saturated pink, bright cyan).
- **Lighting Direction & Quality**: Soft, diffused continuous studio lighting (approx. 5000K daylight-balanced). Soft directional shadow cast to the bottom-right of the product to anchor physical volume and ground contact.
- **Product Styling & Prep**: Products must be stuffed with archival acid-free tissue paper or foam blanks to maintain crisp geometric contours, prevent creasing, and keep zip tracks taut.
- **Branding Governance**: All photographed prototypes must either be **completely unbranded** (ideal for OEM/ODM client presentation) or carry the authorized **ARMANO** brand mark. Never photograph client third-party retail brands without documented written legal release.

### 1.2 Technical Capture Specifications
- **Camera Resolution**: Native sensor capture of minimum **24 Megapixels** (minimum 4000 × 3000 pixels).
- **Lens & Perspective**: 85mm to 105mm macro or prime lens to eliminate barrel distortion and perspective warping.
- **Aperture**: `f/8` to `f/11` for end-to-end edge sharpness and complete depth-of-field.
- **Color Space**: Capture in `Adobe RGB (1998)` or `ProPhoto RGB`; export deliverables as calibrated **sRGB IEC61966-2.1**.
- **Delivery Formats**: Uncompressed 16-bit TIFF or lossless full-resolution PNG masters.
