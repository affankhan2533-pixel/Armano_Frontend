## 1. Executive Release Policy

In accordance with strict luxury B2B presentation standards and client visual feedback:

1. **Automatic Integration Disabled**: The previous threshold/flood-fill cutout pipeline is formally decommissioned.
2. **Authentic Original Photographs Active on Storefront**: Every product in the AR-001 through AR-013 master catalogue is configured to display its genuine, un-corrupted client original photograph from `reference/product-images/`.
3. **Data Registry Decoupling**: In `src/data/products.js`, `cutoutImage` is explicitly set to `null` across all 13 products. Zero defective cutouts are exposed or imported into the live customer-facing storefront.
4. **Trademark Protection**: Under Section 29 of the Indian Trade Marks Act (1999), all 18 third-party branded packshots remain quarantined in `reference/product-images/` and have zero public URLs.
