## 1. Executive Summary & Classification Taxonomy

All 36 original packshots and production photographs supplied in the client repository have been audited across 8 rigorous dimensions:
1. **Original Filename**: Exact file name in `reference/product-images/`.
2. **Documented SKU / Sample Mapping**: Only where verified in `ARMANO MANUFACTURER.pdf` (Pages 1–3) or `additionalSamples.js` (Pages 4–7). No synthetic SKU numbers invented.
3. **Native Resolution**: Pixel width × height.
4. **Staging & Background Type**: Seamless sweep (pure white, black, peach, pink), tabletop, workshop workbench, or factory floor.
5. **Product Visibility & Staging**: Single hero product, paired variant, kit composition, or volume batch.
6. **Visible Third-Party Trademarks**: Identification of unapproved brand marks requiring legal sanitization under Indian trademark governance.
7. **Background Removal Suitability**: Evaluation of edge clarity, contrast with sweep, and suitability for transparent alpha-channel extraction.
8. **Final Processing Status**: Cutout Candidate, WebP Optimization, or Factory Context Archive.
