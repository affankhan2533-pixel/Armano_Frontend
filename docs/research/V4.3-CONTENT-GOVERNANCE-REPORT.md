## 3. Trademark Risk Mitigation & Brand Governance (Task 1 & Task 6)

### 3.1 AR-002 ("RAYS" Wordmark) Analysis
- **Finding**: Client reference photograph `image copy 27.png` displayed a visible blind-debossed wordmark (`"RAYS"`).
- **Legal Context**: Section 29 of the Indian Trade Marks Act (1999) establishes liability for unauthorized commercial use of registered trademarks. Citing Section 29 as an exemption or defense is legally invalid; Section 29 defines what constitutes infringement.
- **Action**: `image copy 27.png` was immediately and completely withdrawn from `public/images/products/AR-002.png`. It was replaced with unbranded camera master `reference/product-images/image copy 23.png` (black velvet evening pouch with gold glitter stripe).
- **Governance Status**: Documented in `src/data/products.js` under `trademarkGovernanceNote`. The asset remains withheld from public production until the client produces written trademark registration proof or an unbranded re-shoot is conducted.

### 3.2 Third-Party Brand Preservation & Client Protection
- All third-party customer brand marks, OEM client brand names, and unverified partner logos remain **hidden** across the site.
- No client case studies are attributed to specific commercial brands without counter-signed publicity releases.
