## 1. Executive Summary

In response to the requirement that the 3D pouch must appear to float directly inside the full hero environment without resembling a product image trapped inside a card, container box, or clipped canvas, ARMANO V6.2 implements a completely borderless WebGL viewport architecture paired with refined satin-matte PBR material physics.

All artificial card boundaries, rounded border strokes, drop-shadow enclosures, and solid-color WebGL stage backdrops have been eliminated. The procedural 3D model now casts a soft, natural diffuse contact shadow directly onto the section's dynamic studio background gradient.

---

## 2. Technical Architecture & Borderless Canvas Implementation

### 2.1 WebGL Renderer Transparency
Previous iterations relied on CSS canvas wrappers that enforced bounded dimensions and non-transparent background fills. In V6.2:
- The Three.js `<Canvas>` component is mounted with explicit alpha enablement:
  ```jsx
  <Canvas
    gl={{
      antialias: true,
      alpha: true,
      powerPreference: 'high-performance',
      toneMapping: THREE.ACESFilmicToneMapping,
      toneMappingExposure: 1.05,
    }}
    style={{ background: 'transparent' }}
    onCreated={({ gl }) => {
      gl.setClearColor(0x000000, 0);
    }}
  >
  ```
- All parent containers (`relative w-full h-[320px] sm:h-[420px] lg:h-[560px] pointer-events-auto`) have zero border, zero box-shadow, and zero background color declarations, permitting the section's dynamic radial gradient to bleed seamlessly beneath the product.

### 2.2 Contact Shadow Falloff Calibration
A critical flaw in bounded 3D presentations is shadow clipping at the plane boundary. In V6.2:
- `ContactShadows` plane scale was expanded from `6.0` to `7.5`.
- Blur was adjusted to `2.8` and opacity calibrated to `0.36`.
- This ensures the ground shadow intensity smoothly decays to absolute zero (`alpha: 0.0`) well before reaching the plane edge, completely preventing square or linear ground clipping artifacts.

## 3. Refined Satin-Matte PBR Material Physics

The previous material formulation exhibited an over-inflated, balloon-like aesthetic caused by excessive cushion loft and specular hot-spots. V6.2 re-engineers the procedural geometry and shader parameters to replicate genuine luxury coated nylon and calfskin leatherette:

| Parameter | V6.1 Formulation | V6.2 Calibrated Physics | Material Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Roughness** | `0.36` | `0.50 – 0.54` | Eliminates plastic glare; replicates luxury matte leatherette. |
| **Clearcoat** | `0.25` | `0.11 – 0.15` | Softens secondary reflection into a gentle satin rim sheen. |
| **Clearcoat Roughness** | `0.10` | `0.40 – 0.44` | Broadens highlight dispersion across curved gussets. |
| **Sheen & Sheen Tint** | `0.40` | `0.52 – 0.55` | Simulates microscopic textile micro-fiber scattering. |
| **Cushion Loft (`zPuff`)** | `0.115` | `0.062` | Reduces diamond bulge by 46%; creates realistic tailored padding. |
| **Seam Bowing (`bow`)** | `0.120` | `0.065` | Tames exaggerated barrel distortion along top and bottom seams. |
| **Metalness (Hardware)**| `0.85` | `0.92` | Restores authentic brushed champagne gold reflectivity. |
